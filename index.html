Why “Wiley Banting”?

Wiley is for smart, resourceful design. Banting honors Sir Frederick Banting, one of the minds behind the discovery of insulin.

Together, the name is our nod to a pioneer in diabetes care and a promise to bring that same ingenuity to gear that is practical, durable, and good-looking enough to carry every day.
